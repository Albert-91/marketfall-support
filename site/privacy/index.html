About Marketfall

Marketfall is an offline, single-player strategy game about a fictional economy. It does not require an account and is not a financial service or investment advice.

Information handled by the app

The current app is designed to store game progress, run history, progression, preferences, and notification settings locally on the device. The current implementation does not include a Marketfall account, online gameplay backend, advertising service, analytics collection service, or in-app purchase service. These statements must be checked against the final release and all included SDKs before publication.

If a player shares a run result, the selected text is passed to the device’s operating-system sharing interface. If reminders are enabled, the app schedules local notifications on the device.

Information sent to the operator

The current app is designed not to send game saves or profile data to a Marketfall server. This must be verified against the exact release binary and its dependencies.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until it is removed through available in-app controls, device settings, or app uninstallation. Device backups may be governed by operating-system settings.

Children

Marketfall is not specifically designed or marketed for children. The final age rating and target-audience declarations must reflect the released game and store questionnaires.

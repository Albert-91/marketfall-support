About Marketfall

Marketfall is an offline, single-player strategy game about a fictional economy. It does not require an account and is not a financial service or investment advice.

Information handled by the app

Marketfall stores game progress, run history, progression, preferences, and notification settings locally on the device. It does not include a Marketfall account, online gameplay backend, advertising, analytics, or in-app purchases.

If you share a run result, the selected text is passed to your device’s operating-system sharing interface. If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device.

Information sent to the operator

Marketfall does not send game saves or profile data to a Marketfall server. The game has no online backend.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it through available in-app controls, device settings, or app uninstallation. Device backups may be governed by your operating-system settings.

Children

Marketfall is not specifically designed or marketed for children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or applicable requirements change. For privacy questions or requests, contact marketfall.support@gmail.com.
